In any legal relationship — in business, in employment, or in other forms of cooperation — disputes that are hard to settle amicably often arise. When conflict happens, a legal consultant (*konsultan hukum*) becomes very important in helping the parties find the best solution within the applicable law. With experience and expertise, a legal consultant can give advice and support throughout the dispute-resolution process, whether through litigation or alternatives such as arbitration and mediation.

## The Role of a Legal Consultant in Resolving Disputes

A legal consultant plays a strategic role in helping individuals and companies settle disputes efficiently. Some of the main roles are:

1. **Giving in-depth legal analysis.** The consultant helps the client understand the legal basis of the dispute, including each party's rights and obligations under the applicable rules. With a thorough analysis, the client can decide on the best step to resolve the dispute with minimal risk.
2. **Building a dispute-resolution strategy.** Every dispute has different characteristics. The consultant helps design the most effective strategy, whether through litigation in court or through alternative dispute resolution such as arbitration, mediation, and negotiation.
3. **Representing the client in negotiation and mediation.** In many cases, an amicable settlement is preferred because it is faster and cheaper than court proceedings. The consultant can represent the client in negotiation and mediation to reach an agreement that is fair to all parties.
4. **Drafting and reviewing legal documents.** Legal disputes often relate to a previously agreed contract. The consultant drafts and reviews legal documents so that no legal gap can harm the client later.
5. **Representing the client in litigation.** If an amicable settlement bears no fruit, litigation becomes the last option. The consultant works with the client's attorney to prepare strong legal arguments and to accompany the client throughout the court process.

## Arbitration as an Alternative to Resolving Disputes

One increasingly used method of dispute resolution is arbitration. Under **Law No. 30 of 1999 on Arbitration and Alternative Dispute Resolution**, arbitration is an effective choice for settling disputes more quickly and more privately than ordinary court proceedings.

**Article 6 paragraph (3) of the Arbitration Law** states that where a dispute cannot be settled directly, the parties may use the services of a mediator or an expert adviser. If the parties do not reach agreement within 14 days, the dispute may proceed to the competent arbitration body.

Here, the legal consultant plays an important part in helping the client understand the arbitration mechanism, build strong legal arguments, and ensure the dispute-resolution process runs according to the applicable rules.

## The Benefits of Using a Legal Consultant

Using a legal consultant to resolve a dispute offers several benefits, among them:

1. **Efficiency in time and cost.** Compared with long and expensive litigation, resolving a dispute through arbitration or mediation with a consultant's help is faster and cheaper.
2. **Confidentiality preserved.** Unlike courts, which are open, arbitration and mediation protect business or family information that is confidential.
3. **A binding and fair decision.** In arbitration, the resulting award is final and binding, which avoids a drawn-out appeal process.
4. **Reducing future legal risk.** The consultant can help the client draft safer contracts and agreements, so that potential disputes are avoided later.

In facing a legal dispute — in business or in any other area — the role of a legal consultant is vital to ensuring the dispute is resolved in the most effective and lawful way. With a consultant's help, you can obtain a fair legal solution, reduce risk, and preserve the stability of your business or working relationship. A competent legal consultant is therefore not only a precaution, but also a long-term strategy for the continuity of your venture and your legal interests.
