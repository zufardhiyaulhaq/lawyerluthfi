{%- comment -%} Attributes for a WhatsApp consultation link. Include it inside an tag, after a space, with topic = umum (general) | perkawinan | waris | tanah | perdata. The href built here is the Indonesian message from _data/whatsapp.yml plus the " (dari website)" tag, URL-encoded (url_encode, then "+" -> "%20", which is byte-identical to encodeURIComponent with !'()* also escaped). It is what a visitor gets before JavaScript runs. The i18n engine reads data-wa and swaps in the active language's message (wa.msg.). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign wa = site.data.whatsapp -%} {%- assign wa_topic = include.topic | default: "umum" -%} {%- assign wa_text = wa.message_id[wa_topic] | append: wa.suffix | url_encode | replace: "+", "%20" -%} href="https://wa.me/{{ wa.number }}?text={{ wa_text }}" data-wa="{{ wa_topic }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener"
